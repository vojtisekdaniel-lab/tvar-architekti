Tvar is an idea-making atelier for public
architecture. Based in Bratislava, we work with
municipalities, mayors, investors, developers and
architecture studios across Europe. We develop
concepts for cultural centres, public housing and
public spaces that people connect to, spanning
from +1M to +20M€ .

We're 8 architects in-house,
plus a network of engineers,
sociologists, landscape people
and one very patient lawyer.
